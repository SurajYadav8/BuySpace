# BuySpace design

## World

Charter-adjacent product marketing on a cool stone ground. Capsule chrome, rounded-22 product panels, equal rail strips, map as a framed stage. Geometric sans. Hedera green used sparingly.

## Tokens

- Background `#EEF1F6`
- Surface `#FFFFFF` / soft `#F7F8FB`
- Ink `#0A0F1A` / muted `#3F4A5C`
- Primary `#0B8F6A`
- Ease `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`
- Panel radius `22px`

## Type

- Display / wordmark: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Body: IBM Plex Sans
- Mono: IBM Plex Mono

## Composition

- Floating capsule nav.
- Hero: Built with + status chip, wide headline, lede + CTAs on one row, then a 3-rail white panel. No empty right column.
- Map sits below as a dark-chrome product stage (`rounded-[22px]`), not loose full-bleed under floating titles.
- Prefer equal rail panels over letter-badge cards.
- "Built with" never "Integrations".
- No em dashes in UI copy.
